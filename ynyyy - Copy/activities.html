Activity 2

Netiquette Case Analysis & Discussion Questions

Scenario: A classmate creates a public post calling out another student for a group project mistake. The post receives negative comments from other peers.

Guide Questions:

  1. Which Core Rules of Netiquette were violated in this situation?
  2. How could the issue have been addressed constructively without public posting?
  3. Draft a short response advising the author on responsible digital citizenship.
